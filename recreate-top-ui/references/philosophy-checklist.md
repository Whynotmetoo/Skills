# philosophy checklist

Before implementation, answer these:

## Layout rhythm
- Is the page narrow and editorial, or wide and product-like?
- How is whitespace distributed?
- Is section rhythm calm or dense?

## Typography
- What creates trust: serif, sans, mono?
- Is hierarchy driven by size, weight, or spacing?
- How strong is the contrast between headings and body?

## Containers
- Are cards structural or decorative?
- Are borders always visible?
- Are sections separated by spacing or dividers?

## Emotional tone
- premium
- technical
- quiet
- editorial
- futuristic
- luxurious
- minimal
- enterprise

## Conversion logic
- where attention flows first
- where CTA hierarchy becomes obvious
- whether trust signals are subtle or explicit
